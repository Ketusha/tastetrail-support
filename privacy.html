TasteTrail Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026
Contact: keti.saginashvili@gmail.com

Information in the game

TasteTrail is an offline cooking game. The current version does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or in-app purchases. The game does not send personal information or game progress to the publisher.

Local progress

Completed levels and star ratings are stored locally on your device to keep your progress when the game is reopened. Local data remains until the app’s data is removed. Device backups are controlled by your device and account settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your question or investigate the reported issue and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Contact us to ask about deleting information you sent to support.

Sharing

The game does not send game-progress data to advertising or analytics providers. Your support messages are not used for advertising.

Your choices

You can remove local game data by deleting the app from your device. Questions about privacy can be sent to the contact email above.

Updates

If the game’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
